StayEase Help & Support Center

Everything you need to know about your bookings, stays, and hosting.

How do I cancel or modify my hotel reservation? +
You can manage, reschedule, or cancel your booking through your account dashboard or by searching your booking using your email or phone number in the Booking History section. Cancellations made at least 48 hours prior to check-in qualify for a full refund.
What payment methods are accepted on StayEase? +
We accept major credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), online bank transfers, and selected local payment gateways in Sri Lanka (LKR & USD supported). All payments are encrypted via secure SSL channels.
How long does it take to process a refund? +
Approved refunds are initiated immediately by our automated system. Depending on your issuing bank, the credited amount will appear in your bank statement within 5 to 10 business days.
What are standard check-in and check-out times? +
Standard check-in is usually at 2:00 PM local time, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you require early check-in or late check-out, you can request it directly with the property host via special requests upon confirmation.
How can I list my villa or hotel property? +
Click on the 'List Your Property' link in the main navigation menu, complete the listing details (title, location, nightly rate, image URL, and amenities), and submit. Your property will go through a quick review before going live.
Are properties verified for guest safety? +
Yes, every property listed on StayEase marked with a 'Verified Luxury' badge undergoes strict identity checks, health & safety compliance, and physical spot-checks to guarantee authentic luxury stays.
